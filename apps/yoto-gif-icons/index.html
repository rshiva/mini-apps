Add an icon to your Yoto

  1. Find an icon above and press Download GIF, or use Download all to get the whole set as a zip.
  2. Open your Make Your Own card in the Yoto app or on the Yoto website and edit its playlist.
  3. Tap the icon next to a track, choose to upload your own icon, and pick the GIF you downloaded.
  4. Save the playlist, then pop the card in the player while it's online so it syncs the new icon.

If the icon shows as a still picture on the player, the upload flattened it to one frame. Uploading through the Yoto API with autoConvert=false keeps the animation.

Questions

Are these Yoto icons free?

Yes. Every icon is free to download and use on your own Make Your Own cards, either one GIF at a time or the whole set as a zip. There is no sign-up.

Do they work on the Yoto Mini and Yoto Player?

Yes. Both players use the same 16×16 pixel display for card icons, so every GIF here is drawn at exactly that size and shows on either player.

Why are the animations so slow and gentle?

Many families use Yoto at bedtime and quiet time. Each icon makes a short movement and then rests, and the colours are softened so the screen glows gently rather than flashing. The child's attention stays on the story.

Why does my icon not move on the player?

Some upload routes convert images to a single frame. If the icon shows as a still picture, upload it through the Yoto API with autoConvert set to false, which keeps every frame of the animation.

Can I use a different icon for each track?

Yes. Every track on a Make Your Own card can have its own icon, so you can give each song or chapter a different animal, vehicle or nursery rhyme.
